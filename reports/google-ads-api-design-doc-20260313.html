Official statement

This system uses the Google Ads API to manage internal advertising campaigns for HTX Bach Gia.

1. Business Model and Intended Use

HTX Bach Gia runs its own internal ERP platform to connect advertising activity with operations, finance, and internal reporting. Google Ads data is used only for the company's own internal campaign monitoring and management workflows. The system is not a public SaaS product and is not offered as a self-service tool for external customers.

  • Authorized internal staff use the tool to review campaign data and business performance.
  • The system links Google Ads data with internal ad account records, ad groups, budgets, and reporting.
  • The tool supports internal campaign oversight and budget decisions, not general public access.

4. Google Ads API Workflow

  • HTX Bach Gia stores Google Ads MCC credentials in the internal Ads Settings area: developer token, client ID, client secret, refresh token, and login customer ID.
  • Each Google Ads customer account is mapped internally to an ERP ad account record.
  • Each relevant Google Ads ad group is mapped to an internal ERP ad group record.
  • The backend runs a scheduled sync to pull Google Ads metrics such as cost, impressions, clicks, average CPC, and average CPM.
  • Synced data is used for internal dashboards, advertising cost tracking, and business reporting.
  • When approved internally, the system can send Google Ads API mutate requests to update mapped campaign budgets.

6. Capabilities in Scope for This Tool

Based on the current implementation, the tool's Google Ads API use is primarily internal Reporting plus limited internal Campaign Management for budget adjustments on mapped campaigns.

Reporting Campaign Management

The tool does not provide public account creation, public campaign creation, or keyword planning features.

7. Campaign Types and Operational Scope

The current workflow is designed for Google Ads customer accounts managed under the HTX Bach Gia MCC and for campaigns that are mapped to internal ERP ad group records for reporting and budget operations. The internal system is used for operational campaign oversight rather than public campaign authoring.

For the application form, the safest scope to declare is the standard campaign types the business manages internally through mapped ad groups, such as Search, Display, Video, and Shopping, only if those are actually in use under the MCC.
