# Canonical Weak Field Dictionary

Each weak field below is read-only evidence context. None is an action command.

| Weak field key | Meaning | Current evidence source | Current limitation | Proposed canonical source | Proposed field shape | DQ impact | Confidence impact | Blocks row? | Future implementation notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `canonical_reserved_quantity` | Stock committed to open orders | Derived from `ordertest2.orderStatus` and `deliverystatuses` | Candidate only, no reservation ledger | Inventory reservation ledger or status policy | `{quantity, source_statuses, effective_at}` | partial | medium ceiling | no | Keep current derived field as downgrade context. |
| `confirmed_incoming_stock_status` | PO status set that counts as confirmed incoming | `purchaseorders.status` candidates `ordered`, `partially_received` | Status semantics not policy-owned | PO status policy registry | `{status_set, owner, effective_from}` | partial | medium ceiling | no | Required before action-grade replenishment evidence. |
| `incoming_stock_quantity_candidate` | Unreceived PO quantity | `purchaseorders.items.quantity - quantityReceived` | Candidate; excludes ambiguous statuses | PO receipt/incoming policy | `{quantity, po_ids, statuses}` | partial | medium ceiling | no | Must remain advisory until confirmed source exists. |
| `days_of_cover_threshold` | Minimum days of stock cover | hardcoded `7` | Not configured or reviewed | Inventory threshold registry | `{value: number, unit: "days"}` | weak if missing | low/medium | yes if absent and no reorder point | Replace implicit constant. |
| `bestseller_rank_window` | Window and rank cutoff for bestseller | derived order window and sorted quantity | No configured window/cutoff | Sales velocity policy | `{window_days, rank_cutoff, min_orders}` | partial | medium ceiling | no | Needed for repeatable BA review. |
| `supplier_quote_approval_status` | Whether supplier quote is approved/accepted | Query reads `approvalStatus` or `status`; current schema lacks these fields | Not guaranteed canonical | Supplier quote workflow/schema | `{status, approved_at, approved_by}` | partial/weak | low if missing | no | Add only in later authorized implementation. |
| `product_cost_history` | Historical product cost baseline | `products.suppliers.appliedPrice`, `importPrice`, `totalCost` | Current-only or partial history | Product cost ledger | `{cost, currency, effective_at, source}` | partial | medium ceiling | no | Needed for high confidence margin pressure. |
| `dealer_price_history` | Dealer/customer price timeline | `quotes.unitPrice` | May be missing or unapproved | Dealer price ledger | `{price, status, effective_from, effective_to}` | partial | low if missing | no | Missing history downgrades supplier cost rows. |
| `dealer_price_lag_threshold` | Maximum tolerated lag after supplier cost change | Derived lag days only | No configured limit | Pricing policy registry | `{days, owner, effective_from}` | weak if missing | low/medium | no | Should not trigger price mutation. |
| `margin_cogs_impact` | Margin/COGS effect from supplier cost or delay | Missing or null in current risk rows | No joined finance impact | Finance COGS/margin ledger | `{amount, percent, period}` | partial | medium ceiling | no | Blocks high confidence, not row emission. |
| `settlement_due_date_source` | Official dealer/agent settlement due date | `ordertest2.agentPaymentDueDate` | Field exists but policy semantics not versioned | Finance settlement policy | `{field, policy_key, effective_from}` | partial | medium ceiling | yes if due date missing | Clarify dealer/agent terminology. |
| `outstanding_balance_formula` | Formula for unpaid settlement amount | Derived from order agent payment fields | Formula is code-level, not policy-owned | Finance formula registry | `{formula_key, inputs, version}` | partial | medium ceiling | yes if no positive amount | Needed for auditable aging. |
| `last_payment_date` | Most recent payment date | `agentstatements.payments` | May be missing or not linked to order | Payment ledger/statement linkage | `{paid_at, amount, source_id}` | partial | low if missing | no | Missing value downgrades only. |
| `collection_owner` | Responsible owner for follow-up | `users.managerId` or payment creator candidate | Candidate ownership only | Finance/CRM ownership mapping | `{owner_id, owner_role, source}` | partial | low if missing | no | Must not become collection action. |
| `agent_statement_linkage` | Link from order to statement | `agentstatements` by agent | Not necessarily order/invoice-level | Statement-order join table/policy | `{statement_id, order_id, basis}` | partial | low if missing | no | Needed to reduce terminology ambiguity. |
| `receivable_payable_terminology_boundary` | Whether row is receivable, payable, settlement, or cash-in | Schema comments clarify payable/settlement | Existing route name is misleading | Finance glossary/policy | `{term, definition, applies_to}` | weak if absent | low/medium | no | Required before strong business conclusion. |
| `canonical_overtime_policy_threshold` | Approved overtime limit | hardcoded 8h/day candidate | Not policy-owned | HR/labor policy registry | `{hours, unit, period, role_scope}` | partial/weak | low if missing | no | Current labor row should remain low confidence. |

| `sla_or_deadline_pressure` | Whether overtime is tied to workload deadlines | null | Missing source | Operations SLA/backlog source | `{sla_count, breached_count, period}` | weak | low if missing | no | Downgrade, not row blocker. |
| `staff_capacity` | Planned capacity or staffing baseline | null | Missing source | HR roster/capacity table | `{capacity_hours, staff_count, period}` | weak | low if missing | no | Required for medium/high confidence. |
| `team_mapping` | Labor group mapping | employee-as-group candidate | Not a team model | User/team registry | `{team_id, members, effective_from}` | partial/weak | low if missing | no | Needed to avoid individual-only inference. |
