How HTX Bach Gia uses the Google Ads API

This page is provided as a clear public explanation of the internal advertising use case connected to the HTX Bach Gia system.

Core statement
This system uses the Google Ads API to manage internal advertising campaigns for HTX Bach Gia.

Intended use

Internal campaign management

The integration is used to support authorized internal staff of HTX Bach Gia. It helps the team review campaign structure, synchronize account and campaign metadata, monitor spend and performance, and compare results with internal operational reporting.

  • Review campaign and ad group structure for internal planning.
  • Track advertising cost and performance metrics for internal reports.
  • Support budget monitoring and operational decision-making.

Access controls

Authorized staff only

Access to the internal system is restricted to authorized users. The public website does not expose Google Ads management features to visitors.

Questions about the integration or compliance review can be sent to vtv.group333@gmail.com.

Data categories

Information used for the integration

Depending on authorized scope, the system may process account identifiers, campaign and ad group metadata, cost information, budget data, and aggregated performance metrics needed for internal reporting.
